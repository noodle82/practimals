SPELLOIDS: SMALL WEBSITE DEFINITION FIX

This patch adds simple offline meanings and examples for all 12 reported words, reuses the existing curriculum glossary for typed lists, fills missing examples on resave, and reports/retries missing dictionary meanings. It does not cover every possible English word: unknown words still use the dictionary or need parent review.

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the five top-level .js/.json app files into the TOP LEVEL of noodle82/practimals, replacing matching files, and commit.
3. Upload the inner tests folder at the repository top level to add the new check. No audio or vendor upload is needed. This instruction file may stay on your computer.
4. After GitHub Pages has finished deploying, reopen the website and refresh it. If the old screen remains, refresh once more so the new website cache takes control. Keep site data: clearing it would remove saved progress.
5. Open Grown-ups and leave your weekly list unchanged. Press Save list & suggest meanings. Do not choose Start a new spelling week just to repair definitions.
6. Check the report: your 12 example words should have 12 meanings and 12 example sentences. Existing nonempty parent text is preserved. Review suggestions against the school meaning.

This patch was tested with the dictionary blocked: all 12 reported words and every built-in curriculum word have local meanings and examples. Existing week ID, progress, monster choices, scrapbook entries and parent text survived a real browser resave. Missing lookup failures and the retry flow were also tested. All 99 automated checks passed. The original v10 source and Apple build configuration were kept separate; an existing Codemagic build is not changed by this download. A later Codemagic build will incorporate the patch if you upload it to its branch.

The live dictionary returned definitions for all the reported examples during diagnosis. The parent's earlier browser failure was not reproduced, so it is not attributed to a particular network cause. Several original results had no example sentence.

SIMPLE MEANINGS AND SENTENCES

ENDEAVOUR
To try hard to do something.
I will endeavour to finish the puzzle.

GLAMOUR
A special beauty or style that seems exciting.
The sparkling dress added glamour to the show.

CONCLUSION
The end of something, or a final decision.
The story had a happy conclusion.

FAVOURITE
The one you like best.
Blue is my favourite colour.

FLAVOUR
The taste of a food or drink.
This ice cream has a strawberry flavour.

JOURNEY
Travelling from one place to another.
Our journey to the beach took an hour.

NEIGHBOUR
Someone who lives near you.
Our neighbour helped us water the plants.

CONFUSION
Feeling mixed up or unsure about something.
There was confusion about whose turn it was.

PERSUASION
Getting someone to agree by talking and giving reasons.
With some persuasion, Dad agreed to play another game.

SUPERVISION
Watching over someone to help keep them safe.
We used the scissors with an adult's supervision.

TELEVISION
A screen that shows programmes and films.
We watched a nature programme on television.

COLOUR
What makes red, blue and yellow look different.
The flower was a bright yellow colour.
