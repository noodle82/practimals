WHAT CHANGED
- Spelloid logo, matching app icon and short intro: Little practice, big confidence.
- Grown-ups gate: square root of 144 (12). A clear Spellings ready — hand to child action saves and checks the list before returning to play.
- Take a photo or choose a picture, rotate if needed, read locally and review/edit the detected words before using them. Printed lists work best; handwriting and poor photos need corrections. Photos are not uploaded to a service. The reader files must be available once before offline use; preparing the full offline app includes them.
- Hear my list is an on/off button: tap again to stop; tap once more to restart from the beginning.
- Three voice choices: Bumble, Fizzlet and Mallow. Speaking speed stays unchanged. Retired choices migrate to retained voices without resetting weekly progress or unlocking a locked choice. Body shapes have different hello, giggle and dance sounds.
- Confirm voice is spaced below the choices. This Week is now Spelling Hub. Hub and module navigation land at the useful content; modules have a prominent teal Back to Spelling Hub button.
- Den has illustrated previews, grey locked games and explicit milestones. One complete core module opens Word Rocket and Cloud Hopper; two open Word Nest; three open Memory Mates; four open Monster Bingo. Previously earned games remain collected; a new set still needs one completed module to reopen the Den. Cycle rewards also remain.
- Rainbow is visible as a locked colour until Badge 8, Rainbow keeper. Once earned it stays available. A colour already confirmed for this set stays fixed; choose rainbow for the next Spelloid.
