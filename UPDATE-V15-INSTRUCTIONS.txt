UPLOAD
1. Extract spelloids-v15-update.zip.
2. Upload ALL extracted contents to the TOP LEVEL of your EXISTING practimals/Spelloids GitHub repository, replacing matching files. Upload the contents, not the ZIP or outer folder. Keep existing files, audio, vendor and fonts.
3. Include the audio folder: the small v13 recording additions are included again, so this single ZIP includes all app changes since v12. Do not delete earlier audio banks.
4. Commit, wait for Pages deployment, then reload. Wait a few seconds and reload again if the older version remains. Do not clear website data: that stores progress.

NURSERY CONTROLS
- Drag the nappy, teddy or dummy onto the baby to put it on. When it is on, tap that SAME item in the menu to take it off. Its selected state and small label change to Tap to take off. A tap on an unused item gives a drag hint rather than putting it on. No extra removal buttons below.
- Putting clothes on stays drag-and-drop. Take clothes off (the hanger) is now a normal tap action.
- Moon pyjamas join the starry onesie and cosy hoodie. These peach pyjamas have a small moon detail and full sleeves/feet. All three remain changeable and save separately for each baby.
- The nappy and teddy on the baby are now lit 3D objects, not flat picture overlays. The smaller teddy uses soft rounded plush shapes. The nappy follows the body, with cloth folds and a CLOSED decorative safety pin. Its saved state remains underneath outfits, and it only shows when undressed. Menu pictures use soft matching shading.
- Existing bottle drinking/burp, strawberry dragging, sleep and care remain. Care does not earn spelling badges or green lights.

GROWN-UP SPEECH HELP
The large voice section is replaced by a CLOSED Speech & offline help section. It retains Read practice questions aloud and Prepare this week's spoken words. The website also offers Prepare speech for offline use; the native app instead explains that those files are already bundled.
These are troubleshooting/preparation tools for new words that are slow or silent, not weekly voice-selection controls. Voice previews and the review link have been removed from this section. The child still chooses the Spelloid voice when creating the weekly character, as before.
