USING THE AUDIO PROGRESS PANEL
Open Grown-ups. The new panel appears directly in the weekly spelling area, above Speech & offline help. Tap Prepare this week's spoken words.
The panel shows the current word, how many words are ready, and a progress bar. Existing recordings/cached words are reused; missing words are generated by the offline engine. Keep the screen open while preparation runs. Initial engine-loading progress also appears in the panel.
Only successfully prepared word audio advances the bar. All words ready produces a full green bar and clear completion message. If some words fail, the bar stays short of full, the panel lists the missing words and offers Check and retry spoken words. Already prepared words are reused on retry.
Leaving the page stops the check without a false completion. Changing the spelling list or voice clears the previous confirmation. After a reload, tap to recheck saved audio rather than relying on an old completion flag.
On the website, you can first use Speech & offline help > Prepare speech for offline use while connected to save the larger files once. The installed iPad app already bundles those files.
This panel prepares the individual spelling words, not every example sentence or spoken introduction. No new recordings or voice changes are included in this update.
