# Practimals v0.3 artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. No CLI fallback or image API key was used. Files are bundled locally in assets/. The original creature images are identity references. Clothing variants are rendered onto each body; the app does not position approximate garment polygons. The baby atlas is displayed as six regular sprite cells.

## babies.png

Transparent background requested: true

Production collectible creature sprite atlas for a children's fantasy learning game. EXACTLY SIX separate super cute BABY creatures, arranged in a precise regular THREE COLUMNS by TWO ROWS grid on a truly TRANSPARENT background, landscape 1536 by 1024. Each grid cell exactly one small full-body baby entirely within its own equally sized square cell, centered with generous padding; no creature touches another cell. Top row left: tiny mint fluffy leaf-eared sprout baby sitting cuddling its tail. Top middle: peach round puff baby with little ears, sleeping peacefully. Top right: lilac bunny baby with huge ears and tiny paws. Bottom row left: bright turquoise baby axolotl with frilly gills and golden eyes. Bottom middle: midnight-blue baby dragon with a luminous tiny star on its forehead and golden little wings. Bottom right: pearly-white baby moon kitten with subtle rainbow fur tips, silver ears and tiny glowing crescent forehead. All have huge irresistible glossy eyes, tiny noses, short little paws and chubby baby proportions. Premium tactile 3D family animated film quality, soft fluffy textures and beautiful soft lighting. These are little babies visually distinct from adult creatures. No clothing, no stands, no eggs, no props, no shadows on background. No text, no grid lines, no numbers, no logos or watermark. Perfectly even grid spacing and consistent subject scale.
